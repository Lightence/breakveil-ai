# BreakVeil Community Conduct

BreakVeil should be a welcoming project for people with every level of technical experience.

Participants are expected to:

- Be patient, respectful and constructive.
- Explain technical ideas without belittling beginners.
- Critique code and decisions, not people.
- Respect privacy and never post another person's job-search data or credentials.
- Avoid harassment, threats, discrimination and unwanted personal attention.
- Accept reasonable moderation intended to keep the project safe.

Unacceptable material may be edited or removed, and people who repeatedly harm the community may be excluded from project spaces. Serious conduct concerns should be reported privately using the method in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
